The origin

Every event we run, runs on eventOS.

We got tired of planning events across ten disconnected tools — so we built one. Every module inside eventOS started as something we built for a real job: budgets we tracked, floor plans we mapped, exhibitor intelligence we mined, schedules we ran.

They earned their place one production at a time. eventOS is what happened when we finally put them together. It got too good to keep to ourselves.
